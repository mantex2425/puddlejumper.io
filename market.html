What Uber drivers actually keep per hour

Take the fare, subtract the miles at whatever your car really costs, and divide by the time the whole job took — the drive to the pickup as well as the trip. That is what you keep per hour. Set the two sliders above to your own numbers and the chart recounts every logged Houston offer against them.

Why the fare is not the hourly rate

A fare says nothing about how long it takes or how far you drive to earn it. Two offers paying the same can be twenty minutes apart in work and several dollars apart in cost. The car is paid out of the same fare, whether or not the card mentions it, which is why an offer that looks fine can leave very little behind.

What DSI means on an offer

DSI scores the middle offer against your line. 100 means it cleared. The green bars are the share of offers that reached 100. A day can sit near 100 and still clear almost none, because the line is the middle offer, not the count.
